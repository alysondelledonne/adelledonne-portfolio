PassPhrase Pal

A website that makes memorable, unique passwords, with a Rust core compiled to WebAssembly.

  • Every password is made in your browser with its cryptographic generator. Nothing is sent to a server or stored.
  • Counts every password that fits your rules and picks one uniformly, instead of guessing and retrying.
  • Property-based tests over thousands of random settings, plus 27 page tests.
  • GitHub Actions CI on macOS, Windows, and Linux, deployed to GitHub Pages.

_02 · Oct 2026

Messy Data Reconciliation Pipeline

Give it a target schema and a pile of messy files. It returns one clean, deduplicated table where every value traces back to its source, and it flags what needs a person's review.

  • Ingests spreadsheets, PDFs and scans, emails, Word documents, notes, and JSONL logs, keeping each record's exact source location.
  • Claude extracts records with structured outputs, returning a confidence score and quoted evidence for every field. Hard documents escalate from Haiku to Opus, and every call is cached and costed.
  • Rules and confidence decide what's approved automatically and what needs review, with a reason for each flag. Fuzzy matching with a lookalike-name guard reconciles organizations across sources.
  • A Streamlit review app lets a person approve or correct records, with a full audit trail in Postgres.
  • An eval harness scores every stage against generated ground truth, and GitHub Actions fails the build if a score drops below baseline.

_03 · Sep 2026
Private repo

Shot Idea Orchestrator

An AI pipeline that turns a small brand's shot ideas into approved product photos, without asking the team to open a new app.

  • A shot idea typed into the team's Google Sheet, or sent with a Slack command, starts a request. Edits settle in Postgres for 30 seconds before anything is generated.
  • Claude expands the idea into a detailed prompt, then an image-generation model creates two candidates from the product's own catalog photo, queued through Redis and RQ.
  • The approver picks Approve, Regenerate, or Reject from a Slack card on her phone. Approved images land in Google Drive, and the founder gets a "ready to publish" message.
  • Budget guardrails cap spend per request and per month, and a /report command shows live spend and coverage.
  • Deployed on Railway, with 40 pytest tests passing against a real Postgres 16 database.
