About me

I'm a consultant at Red Hat, where I help enterprises in automotive, financial services, and the nonprofit world build platforms on OpenShift, RHEL, Quay, and Ansible Automation Platform. Most of my days are hands-on: designing cluster networking and storage, setting up RBAC and compliance scanning, and automating the work so nobody has to do it by hand twice.

I like the whole arc of a project, from the first whiteboard session with a customer to the day their workloads run in production. Recently that has meant leading a mixed team of eight on a bank's OpenShift platform, which earned an internal Top Gun award, and moving a customer's virtual machines from VMware to OpenShift Virtualization.

Outside of client work I run an OpenShift cluster at home and write bits & gloss, a newsletter that explains how AI works without the jargon.
